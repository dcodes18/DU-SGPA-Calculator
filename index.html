Disclaimer: This calculator provides an estimated grade based on the prescribed percentage benchmarks. Under Delhi University's relative grading framework, grade cut-offs may be determined using the university cohort's mean (X̄) and standard deviation (σ), or the corresponding fixed percentage benchmark, whichever is lower. For example, the cut-off for a D grade is the lower of X̄ − σ or 30%, while for a C grade it is the lower of X̄ − 0.5σ or 40%. Since the university-wide X̄ and σ values are unavailable, your officially awarded grade and SGPA may differ from this estimate.
